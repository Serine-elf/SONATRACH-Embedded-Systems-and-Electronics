# SONATRACH-Embedded-Systems-and-Electronics
Embedded systems and electronics internship at SONATRACH, involving PCB design for a smart home alarm system using smoke, water leakage, and ultrasonic sensors, alongside electronics, power electronics, and simulation.
As part of my engineering studies in Automatic Control and Systems Engineering, I had the opportunity to complete an internship at SONATRACH, where I explored embedded systems, electronics, and power electronics.

One of the main projects I worked on was the design of a PCB for a smart home alarm system. The project involved integrating different sensors, including a smoke detector, a water leakage sensor, and an ultrasonic sensor, to create a system capable of monitoring potential hazards and improving home security.

I worked on the PCB design using Eagle and also explored other design tools such as KiCad and EasyEDA. I additionally used MATLAB/Simulink and Proteus to explore electronic simulation and system analysis.

Technical Skills & Tools:
This experience allowed me to develop my knowledge of embedded systems, electronic circuit design, PCB development, and power electronics.
I gained experience with Eagle, KiCad, EasyEDA, MATLAB/Simulink, and Proteus, while learning more about sensor integration and the relationship between hardware and embedded technologies.

My Interests:
I am particularly interested in embedded systems, electronics, robotics, and intelligent hardware solutions. I enjoy designing electronic systems and exploring how sensors, microcontrollers, and software can work together to solve real-world problems.
